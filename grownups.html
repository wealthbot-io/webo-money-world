For grown-ups

Webo's Money World is a free, playful introduction to money for children aged about 6 to 10. Twelve short lessons, one idea each, built on the CFPB Money as You Grow milestones and the Jump$tart national standards. Everything is metaphor and simple arithmetic: no real money, no products, no advice.

The lessons, and one thing to try at home

In the order children play them. Each card says what the lesson teaches and gives a small, real-world way to keep the conversation going.

Privacy and safety

  • No accounts, no names, no email. Progress is saved on the device. A child can move stars to another device with a short random code; the code holds only which lessons are done, nothing about the child.
  • Ask Webo is filtered both ways. What a child types and what Webo replies both pass a content check tuned for young children before anything is shown. Off-topic questions get a friendly nudge back to money. If the check itself is unavailable, Webo asks to try again rather than answering unchecked.
  • Nothing to buy, nothing to click through. No ads, no in-app purchases, no links inside the game.
  • Still a demo. A child-safety review and legal sign-off are on the list before any wider public launch. Please play alongside younger children as you would with any chat feature.

Who made this

Webo's Money World is a free project from wealthbot.io. It is for learning only and does not offer financial, investment, or tax advice to anyone, grown-ups included.
